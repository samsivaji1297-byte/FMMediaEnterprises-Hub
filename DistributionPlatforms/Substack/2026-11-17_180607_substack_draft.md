## 1. The Psychological Blind Spot

When you receive a steady salary, your brain tags it as *certainty*. Two well‑studied biases kick in:

* **Status‑quo bias** – the preference for the current state over any change.  
* **Sunk‑cost fallacy** – the feeling that the time and effort already invested makes quitting feel like a loss.

Together they create *Opportunity‑Blindness*: a tunnel‑vision that discounts any potential upside, no matter how compelling.

## 2. Why Conventional Advice Fails

Most career‑growth articles tell you to “network more” or “learn a new skill.” Those tactics assume you already *see* the upside. If your brain is discounting it, you’ll never invest the energy to follow through. The result is endless hustle with flat earnings.
