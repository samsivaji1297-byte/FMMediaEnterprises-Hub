# The Effort‑Value Illusion: Rewire Your Pricing to Capture Real Worth  

> **Executive Summary:** The Effort‑Value Illusion makes you equate hours worked with price, causing chronic under‑payment. By shifting to outcome‑driven pricing and a simple Value‑vs‑Effort matrix, you can command higher rates without adding work.  

## The Core Friction & Root Cause  
Freelancers and solo operators obsess over “how many hours I’m putting in” because their brain rewards visible labor. This creates a *systemic failure*: pricing decisions are anchored to effort, not to the measurable value delivered to the client. Conventional advice (track billable hours, raise rates slowly) only reinforces the bias and leaves the operator stuck in a low‑margin grind.

1. **Map Your Core Deliverables** – List every service you sell.
