# DIGITAL WAR ROOM // VIRALITY & HOOK DOSSIER

## 1. CORE Hook CONSTRUCTS (0–3 SECONDS)
*The primary goal of the hook is pattern interruption and high visual friction. Never open with a greeting or context.*

* **The Exposure Hook**: "You are losing [X hours / $X] every single week because you still do [Action] manually."
* **The Callout Hook**: "If you are still managing [Process] by hand in 2026, your pipeline is already leaking."
* **The Paradox Hook**: "The hardest workers in [Industry] are getting outperformed by single-file Python scripts."
* **The Negative Constraint**: "Stop building bloated systems until you fix this one operational bottleneck."

---

## 2. NARRATIVE & PACING RULES
1. **Pacing**: Maximum 3 seconds per scene/sentence. High kinetic rhythm.
2. **Word Count Limits**: 
   * Hook: Under 10 words.
   * Total Voiceover Script: 35–45 words max.
3. **Tone**: Sovereign, authoritative, zero-fluff, direct-response operator mindset.
4. **Visual Prompts**: Cyberpunk, dark charcoal UI, high-contrast terminal code, obsidian background, cyan/gold text overlays.

---

## 3. CALL TO ACTION (CTA) CONSTRUCTS
*Drive high-intent comments for direct-response automation sweeps.*

* "Comment **PIPELINE** to deploy the automated war room architecture."
* "Comment **OPERATOR** to get the full script breakdown sent to your DMs."
* "Comment **VAULT** to access the direct execution templates."
