**Post 1/4**
The great irony of working at an agency is that it systematically strips you of yours. 

When your day is run by chaotic Jira boards, reactive Slack channels, and client tantrums, you aren't building a career. 

You are training your brain in learned helplessness. 🧵

**Post 2/4**
Every micro-crisis you solve drains your prefrontal cortex. By 5 PM, your brain has zero cognitive reserve left to plan an exit. 

You don’t stay at a low-paying, toxic job because you lack ambition. 

You stay because your environment has put you in a chronic "Survival Deficit."

**Post 3/4**
To break this loop, stop trying to optimize the chaos. You cannot organize your way out of a fundamentally broken business model. 

Instead, implement "Cognitive Offshoring":
• Dial back daily output to 75%
• Treat job hunting as your primary, high-agency client
• Save your best mental energy for *your* exit

**Post 4/4**
If you have advanced skills (or a Master's degree) and you're stuck running PPC or marketing accounts for micro-managers, your problem isn’t your credentials. 

It’s your operating system. 

Stop reacting to their crises. Start orchestrating your pivot.
