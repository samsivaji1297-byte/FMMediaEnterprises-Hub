## The Core Friction & Root Cause  
The raw forum posts reveal a single, painful image: a founder staring at a €10 k balance and feeling “paralysis.” This isn’t indecision; it’s a neuro‑economic response. Loss‑aversion spikes dopamine when a concrete amount is at stake, while imagined upside generates only a faint, diffuse reward signal. ## Step‑By‑Step Execution Protocol  

1. **Identify a Micro‑Revenue Hook** – One product/service you can deliver in ≤30 days.  
2. **Create a One‑Page Pre‑Sale Funnel** – Clear headline, price, limited‑seat CTA. Use Carrd, Softr, or a simple HTML page.  
3. **Capture Deposits** – Set a low entry price (€50‑€200). Connect Stripe or PayPal for instant payment.  
4. **Validate Demand** – If you hit 3–5 deposits, you have a *validated* market.  
5. **Fund the Build** – Use the collected cash to create the first unit (prototype, content, service delivery).  
6. **Deliver & Upsell** – Fulfill the pre‑orders, then open the full‑price offering.  
7. **Iterate** – Scale the pre‑sale loop, increasing price and decreasing seat count each round.
