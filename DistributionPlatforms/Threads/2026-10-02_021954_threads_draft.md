**REEL HOOK:**
You aren’t "stuck" in your career because you lack options. 
You’re stuck because you’re addicted to certainty before taking action.

**REEL CAPTION:**
Low agency isn't laziness—it's a coping mechanism for fear. High agency isn't having all the answers; it's being comfortable taking micro-actions in the dark. Stop waiting for permission or a perfect roadmap to fix your career. Here is how you reclaim agency today 👇

---

**POST 1/4:**
Low agency isn’t laziness—it’s an addiction to certainty. 

You tell yourself you’re stuck because you don't have a 5-year plan. But over-analyzing without executing is just sophisticated anxiety. 

You don't need a strategy; you need a bias toward friction.

**POST 2/4:**
People with high agency don't have better opportunities or secret insights. 

They simply tolerate ambiguity better than you do. 

While you're waiting for explicit permission or a guaranteed outcome, they are running small, messy experiments in the real world.

**POST 3/4:**
If you feel trapped in your job, break the loop:

1. Stop asking "What is the perfect next step?"
2. Ask "What is a low-risk, high-asymmetry action I can take in 15 minutes?"
3. Reach out directly to a target peer, build a public proof-of-work asset, or pitch an unprompted project.

**POST 4/4:**
The antidote to a career rut isn't a new resume—it's a shift in default behavior.

Low agency waits for momentum to act. 
High agency creates momentum by acting through discomfort.

Pick one point of friction today and push through it.
