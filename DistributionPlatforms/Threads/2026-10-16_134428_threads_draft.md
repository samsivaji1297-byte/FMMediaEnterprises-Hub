**REEL HOOK:**  
You aren't refactoring your code at 2 AM because your standards are high.  
You're refactoring because an unreleased project can't fail.

**REEL CAPTION:**  
Perfectionism isn't about quality control—it's an emotional shield against market feedback. Here is how to break the launch loop before feature creep kills your product. 🧵

---

**POST 1**  
The 90% completion mark is where projects go to die.  
  
Notice how the last 10% suddenly requires 3 extra features, a complete visual redesign, and a custom analytics engine?  
  
That’s not product discipline. That’s your ego looking for a place to hide.

**POST 2**  
As long as your project stays on localhost, it retains infinite potential.  
  
It could be a multi-million dollar app. It could land you your dream job.  
  
The moment you press "Deploy," reality collapses that fantasy into a metric. Launching forces you to exchange imaginary potential for real feedback.

**POST 3**  
We convert external risk into internal labor.  
  
• External risk = Silence from users (uncontrollable)  
• Internal labor = Tweaking CSS and refactoring database queries (controllable)  
  
You choose the labor every time because working feels like progress, while deploying feels like exposure.

**POST 4**  
Reframe Version 1.0:  
  
V1 is not a demonstration of your ultimate skill level.  
V1 is a low-cost diagnostic tool designed to test if the world cares about the core problem.  
  
If you aren't mildly embarrassed by the first version, you launched too late. Strip the scope, kill the extra features, and hit deploy.
