**Direct Answer:** The bottleneck is the “perfection‑paralysis tax” – a loss‑aversion driven fear that any imperfection equals personal failure. It exists because self‑esteem is tied to product quality, inflating perceived risk. The fix: impose a hard timebox, ship a minimal viable feature to a private beta, and iterate on real feedback.

> **Executive Summary:**

## The Core Friction & Root Cause
Entrepreneurs constantly tell themselves they need a “perfect” product before the world sees it. That narrative fuels a *loss‑aversion tax*: the brain over‑weights the imagined cost of negative judgment. Conventional advice—more planning, longer checklists—only adds steps to the same mental loop, never addressing the fear itself.

## Step‑By‑Step Execution Protocol
1. **Identify ONE Core Value Claim** – e.g., “helps freelancers book 2 extra clients per week.”  
2. **Draft a One‑Screen MVP** – strip everything else away.
