# The Cognitive Switching Cost Trap: Collapse Your Tool Chaos into a Single Decision Engine

> **Executive Summary:** Choice‑paralysis stems from the *cognitive switching cost* and *decision‑fatigue tax* generated by sprawling productivity stacks. By consolidating to a single decision engine and automating once, you eliminate open loops, slash mental load, and reclaim hours of focused work.

## The Core Friction & Root Cause
Redditors repeatedly scream about being “paralyzed by the need to have the perfect app.” The friction isn’t a lack of willpower; it’s a **systemic overload**. Every extra app forces the brain to rebuild context, creating open loops that occupy working memory and inflate anxiety. Traditional advice—brain‑dump → prioritize—only surfaces more items, magnifying the problem instead of solving it.

## Step‑By‑Step Execution Protocol
1. **Audit Your Stack** – Export a list of every productivity app you touch weekly.  
2. **Score & Prioritize** – Rate each on *frequency* (1‑5) and *unique value* (1‑5). Keep the top‑scoring 1‑2 tools.  
3. **Select a Decision Engine** – Recommended: Notion (flexible DB + native automations) or ClickUp (task‑centric + integrations).  
4. **Migrate Data** – Use CSV/JSON imports or native connectors to bring notes, tasks, and timelines into the hub.  
5. **Build a Single Automation** – Create one Zapier/Make scenario that handles recurring actions (e.g., email → task, calendar → reminder).  
6. **Close Open Loops** – Set a daily 5‑minute “Inbox Sweep” in the hub to process any stray items.  
7. **Run a 7‑Day Single‑Tool Sprint** – Work exclusively within the hub; track mental bandwidth saved in a simple log.  

*Result:* You open **one** dashboard each morning, click once, and the system executes the rest. No more tab‑hunting, no more decision fatigue.
