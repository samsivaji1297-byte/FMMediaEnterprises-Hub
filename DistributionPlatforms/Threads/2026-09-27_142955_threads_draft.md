**REEL HOOK:**
Stop saving side hustle ideas you’re never going to launch. 
You aren't afraid of failing—you’re addicted to the safety of potential.

**CAPTION:**
Your business idea is 100% successful right now because it only exists in your head. The moment you launch, you risk contact with reality. Here is how to break the paralysis cycle before another year slips by.

---

**POST 1:**
You’re not actually afraid of starting a business. You’re afraid of losing the comforting lie that you *could* be rich/successful if you tried.

In your head, the idea is flawless. In reality, it will be messy. You delay launching to protect your ego from being tested.

---

**POST 2:**
The psychological trick your brain uses is called Scope Inflation.

Instead of asking: "Can I get 1 stranger to pay me $20 today?"
You ask: "Am I ready to build a $1M brand, register an LLC, design a logo, and quit my job?"

You make the task so big that non-action feels responsible.

---

**POST 3:**
Shift your metric from "Success vs. Failure" to "Velocity of Contact."

Amateurs try to guarantee success before touching the market. 
Pros reduce the time between having an idea and putting it in front of a real human to buy.

Friction vanishes when the scope shrinks.

---

**POST 4:**
Lower the stakes until your ego doesn't care.

Don't "launch an art business." DM 3 colleagues and offer a custom portrait for $50.
Don't "build a SaaS company." Pre-sell a Google Sheet template on X.

Kill the grand vision. Start making reality give you feedback.
