## The Core Friction & Root Cause  
Most productivity frameworks assume you’ll *remember* to hit “run” after planning. In reality, the brain discounts future effort and adds a mental activation cost to every manual step. That cost creates an invisible lag—your plan sits idle until you muster enough motivation, which often never arrives. Conventional tools (checklists, timers) merely surface the problem; they don’t remove the cost.

1. **Map Your Cue** – List the exact digital event that signals you’re ready (e.g., a new markdown file in `~/projects`).  
2. **Define the Action** – Write a small script or select a pre‑built Zap that performs the next step (formatting, uploading, notifying).  
3. **Choose an Automation Layer** –  
   * *Zapier* for no‑code users (free tier supports 100 tasks/mo).
